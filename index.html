Climate risk in a coffee supply chain

A TCFD-aligned analysis of how climate change could reduce coffee supply and gross profit, from sourcing regions to business impact.

  • Built regional climate-stress indicators for near- and mid-century scenarios

  • Built an Excel calculator so decision-makers can test the scenarios themselves
