01 — About me

Meet Amaka.

My mission

To make climate and ESG data trustworthy enough that companies and banks can act on it.

Top skills

Climate riskFinanced emissions (PCAF)Carbon accountingCSRD/ESRSScenario analysisPower BIPythonSQLData validation

I'm Manager Sustainability Data and Analytics at STILL, part of KION Group, in Hamburg. I'm an agricultural economist with an M.Sc. from the University of Hohenheim, and I hold the GARP Sustainability and Climate Risk certificate.

Before STILL, I calculated Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions for a coffee group with 42 subsidiaries, from farm-level inputs to transport, and supported its TCFD, CDP, EcoVadis and CSRD/ESRS reporting. Alongside my work, I build independent projects on climate risk and financed emissions.
